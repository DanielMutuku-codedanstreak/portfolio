About Me

I'm a software engineer based in Nairobi, Kenya. I build mobile apps with Flutter, mostly for products that connect software to the physical and financial world: EV chargers, M-Pesa payments and merchant wallets.

I began in design and freelance work, creating logos, school emblems, flags and websites for small organisations. That taught me to care about spacing, clarity and building things people can actually use. After a BSc in Software Engineering, several certifications and a series of early developer roles, I moved into mobile engineering.

Outside work, I mentor junior engineers through code reviews and debugging sessions, and I help organise local youth initiatives around digital skills.

Selected Work

Mobile App • EV Charging Geviton Limited · 2025 – Present

SafiCharge

A production Flutter app for EV drivers in Kenya. Drivers find chargers on a map, start and follow charging sessions, and pay with M-Pesa or a wallet. I'm the primary mobile developer, working alongside another Flutter developer and a backend engineer who owns the charger network and the API.

  • Station discovery on an interactive map with clustering and cached tiles.
  • Charging session screens that follow a session live.
  • Payment flows for M-Pesa STK push, paybill and wallet, with status tracking.
  • Support tickets and chat, transaction history and RFID fob management.
  • Offline caching and Sentry error reporting for patchy mobile networks.
