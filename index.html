About Me

I'm a software engineer in Nairobi, Kenya. I build mobile apps with Flutter, mostly for products where software meets the real world: electric vehicle chargers, M-Pesa payments, and merchant wallets.

I didn't start in engineering. My first income came from design and freelance work: logos, school emblems and flags, and websites for small organisations. That is where I learned to care about spacing, clarity and delivering something a client can actually use. A BSc in Software Engineering, a few certifications and a handful of early dev roles later, I now work as a mobile engineer.

This page is that path, not just the highlights. The CV has the formal version.

Selected Work

Mobile App • EV Charging Geviton Limited · 2025 – Present

SafiCharge

A production Flutter app for EV drivers in Kenya. Drivers find chargers on a map, start and follow charging sessions, and pay with M-Pesa or a wallet. I am the main mobile developer, working with one other Flutter developer and a backend engineer who owns the charger network and the API.

  • Station discovery on an interactive map with clustering and cached tiles.
  • Charging session screens that follow a session live.
  • Payment flows for M-Pesa STK push, paybill and wallet, with status tracking.
  • Support tickets and chat, transaction history and RFID fob management.
  • Offline caching and Sentry error reporting for patchy mobile networks.
